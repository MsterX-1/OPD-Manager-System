# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Hospital staff including Receptionists, Doctors, Department Staff (Labs/Imaging), and Administrators. They use this in high-pressure clinical environments where speed, clarity, and reliability are paramount.

## Product Purpose
An Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Hospital System tailored for Outpatient Department (OPD) management. It tracks real-time patient queues, doctor encounters, department referrals, and system-wide analytics to streamline hospital operations.

## Brand Personality
Professional, authoritative, ultra-reliable, and highly legible. It should feel like a state-of-the-art medical instrument—not a trendy startup app.

## Anti-references
- AI-generated clichés (glassmorphism, gradient text, excessive blurs).
- Generic SaaS templates (hero-metric templates, overly playful UI, rounded bubbly aesthetics).
- Consumer-grade apps with low information density.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over cleverness**: Data must be instantly readable; no ambiguous icons without labels.
- **High information density**: Medical professionals need to see a lot of data at a glance without feeling overwhelmed.
- **Restrained elevation**: Use subtle depth to indicate hierarchy, avoiding flashy shadows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Must support WCAG AA standards. High contrast for clinical environments. Full support for RTL (Arabic) languages, ensuring robust bidirectional layout mirroring.
